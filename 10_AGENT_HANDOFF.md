## Current Product Direction
**Muamer-level simplicity + WhatsApp-first field capture + supervisor review + finance review + multiple advances + weekly close + BOQ Lite + fast IPCs + client portal.**

## What We Are Not Building
- full ERP
- full accounting
- HR/payroll
- large inventory
- CRM
- native app in MVP

## Critical Product Logic
- WhatsApp is capture, not final approval.
- Original evidence is preserved.
- Supervisor corrects operational interpretation.
- Accountant sees full history and does financial treatment.
- One employee can receive multiple advances before previous ones close.
- Weekly Close should reduce manual collection.
- IPC is based on contract/progress, not expenses.
- AI never finalizes financial actions by itself.
