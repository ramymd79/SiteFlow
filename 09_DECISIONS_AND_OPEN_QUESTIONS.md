# Decisions & Open Questions

## Decisions Already Taken
- Product is not advances-only.
- Product is not ERP.
- Muamer simplicity is a reference, not something to clone blindly.
- WhatsApp is core.
- Web Dashboard is core.
- Native app is not MVP.
- Multiple open advances for same user are allowed.
- Original voice/photo/text must remain available.
- Supervisor can correct operational interpretation before finance.
- Finance sees full edit history.
- Accountant performs final financial treatment.
- Weekly Close is core.
- Client IPC is core.
- Client Portal is core.
- AI proposes; human approves.

## Kill Criteria
- لا نجد 3 شركات تدفع.
- لا يستخدم الفريق WhatsApp capture بعد التدريب.
- أسبوع الإقفال لا يتحسن.
- المستخلص لا يوفر وقتًا حقيقيًا.
- الحاجة الفعلية تنحرف إلى ERP كامل.
